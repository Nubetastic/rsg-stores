# Door Lock Ignore Export

Temporarily ignore one store's door locks for the current client. Use the location ID from the list below.

```lua
exports['rsg-stores']:SetIgnoreLocation('val_general_store')
exports['rsg-stores']:SetIgnoreLocation(nil) -- restore normal door locking
```

## Locations

- `rho_general_store` - Rhodes General Store
- `val_general_store` - Valentine General Store
- `str_general_store` - Strawberry General Store
- `stden_general_store` - Saint Denis General Store
- `tum_general_store` - Tumbleweed General Store
- `arm_general_store` - Armadillo General Store
- `blk_general_store` - Blackwater General Store
- `van_general_store` - Van Horn General Store
- `val_gunsmith` - Valentine Gunsmith
- `tum_gunsmith` - Tumbleweed Gunsmith
- `stden_gunsmith` - Saint Denis Gunsmith
- `rho_gunsmith` - Rhodes Gunsmith
- `ann_gunsmith` - Annesburg Gunsmith
- `val-saloon` - Valentine Saloon
- `rho-saloon` - Rhodes Saloon
- `old-saloon` - Old Light Saloon
- `tumble-saloon` - Tumbleweed Saloon
- `Doctor-StDenis` - Saint Denis Doctor
- `Doctor-Valentine` - Valentine Doctor
